# P1.5.3g1 durable parked staging catalog (local)

Migration 20 adds a staging catalog scoped to root/file identity and the
normalized GUID sibling path. Registration requires the exact still-active
apply intent and bound root in a FULL transaction before that intent is
abandoned/deferred. File/directory kind, target, first parked time, length/hash
and native change stamp are captured under an existing exclusive handle or a
verified reopened guard. Unchanged fingerprints preserve age across restart
and clock rollback; newly settled fingerprints renew grace. Protected prepared
objects remain excluded from resume. This catalog does not authorize deletion.

Resume freezes identity, rejects hardlinks/delete-pending files, checks kind,
length, native change time and whole-prefix hash, then lets the assembler verify
each desired chunk again. Changed objects remain untouched and a fresh target
can use independently published blocks. Legacy pointers retain safe verified
reuse without granting age cleanup; they cannot bypass a cataloged object's
protection. Malformed UTF-8 pointers are skipped with private diagnostics.
Cancellation closes probe guards and leaves unfinished ownership intents
recoverable. GUID siblings may exceed the 4,096-character wire-target bound;
the validated parent/target and internal 4,352-character bound are preserved.

Twelve Storage additions cover durable reopen/abandonment, live-intent/root
binding, caller-buffer isolation, writer-queued freezing, age renewal/rollback,
case aliases, kind/target stability, protection, malformed paths, cancellation,
long siblings and v19 backup/legacy preservation. Ten native Engine additions
cover restart/re-park/resume, changed data and legacy bypass, corrupted prepared
bytes, hardlinks through both resume paths, legacy compatibility, cancellation
guard release, canceled catalog commit, malformed pointers and directory
transition staging. Three older upgrade fixtures now actually remove the new
table before reconstructing their legacy schemas. The existing cancellation
reuse fixture seeds its owned bytes before recovery records the fingerprint;
a separate new test proves writes after cataloging survive.
