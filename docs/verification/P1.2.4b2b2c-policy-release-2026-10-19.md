# P1.2.4b2b2c: local policy reference retirement

The host-owned `HistoryPolicy` authenticates legacy/current history under the
pinned root and holds the filesystem gate followed by the reference gate.
Pending applications defer the operation. Exact marker bytes are compared
inside FULL transactions before policy retirement. Private expiry uses the
history observation time; early capacity retirement requires a completed,
root/source-bound witness prepared no earlier than the hold. A prepared,
future-completed, older or numerically reused archive witness cannot retire
newer history. Public expiry additionally requires an authenticated local
publication or tombstone for its conflict location; unobserved/unpublished
copies remain protected.

Migration 15 records root/source-bound archive policy releases. No source is
released while a history hold remains or a native retirement is pending.
Detached sources can expire by explicit age, but absence alone is insufficient.
New history reuse cancels an earlier release atomically with its new hold;
SQLite failure rolls both changes back. Provenance, active content and staged
prefixes remain stored for later bounded metadata cleanup. Reference refresh
excludes released archive manifests/prefixes, while current, needed and other
history references remain independent. Policy retirement does not delete
archive files, block files or user data. Old active snapshots remain protective
until a new serialized refresh activates; they never authorize a later sweep.
