with a service instance, monotonic sequence, explicit production availability,

signed offset. This checkpoint implements the protocol and frozen mapping only;
named-pipe hosting, authenticated client connection and rendered UI remain WIP.

identities, duplicate sessions, zero sequence and empty service identity. The

collections and identity buffers; diagnostic string output redacts identities.

checks for Contracts and its tests are clean without load warnings or truncation.
