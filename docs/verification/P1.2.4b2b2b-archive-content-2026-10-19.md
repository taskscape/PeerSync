# Verified archive content manifests — 2026-10-19

Migration 12 adds root/source/native-file-bound archive content manifests.
Capture verifies an independent private file under a no-write/no-delete-sharing
read guard, hashes and chunks its complete indexed bytes, and registers copied
blocks through the ordinary durable ownership path. Bounded batches of at most
1,000 contiguous chunks stage invisibly; FULL activation follows complete
length/hash verification and block registration. A 100,000-chunk budget bounds
manifest construction. Incomplete or failed replacements retain the previous
active manifest, while abandoned staging is removed only by the next verified
build of that same source.

Recovery can verify every staged independent block and the complete whole-file
hash, then FULL-activate a complete manifest after restart even when the original
archive has disappeared. Missing/corrupt blocks, incompatible roots and partial
manifests do not become active. Reference snapshots include active archive
content and staged prefixes, preserving their hashes while work is pending.
Stable provenance source IDs carry coverage across archive-row removal and
prevent unrelated reused archive IDs from inheriting coverage. Compatible
legacy coverage migrates; foreign-root coverage remains uncovered.

Authenticated history manifests protect their original chunk partitions without
unnecessary archive recapture. A valid raw archive can now become a verified
source; missing, changed, locked, linked, reparse or foreign-root sources remain
uncovered and emit count/code-only deferrals. Existing active content remains
protection when its private location later changes or disappears. Neither
coverage nor a content manifest authorizes any archive or blob deletion.

replacement after database reopening, five capture boundaries, complete-stage
recovery without the original, partial-prefix protection, unavailable/changed/
