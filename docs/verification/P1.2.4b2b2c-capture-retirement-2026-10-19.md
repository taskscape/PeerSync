# P1.2.4b2b2c: local incomplete capture retirement

Migration 17 adds FULL-durable retirement of proof-bound incomplete capture
temporaries. The engine authenticates current/needed/history/archive coverage
under both maintenance gates, verifies the pinned root and independent proof
and data IDs, then records the exact proof fields, observed prefix length/hash,
snapshot and age before native deletion. Only strict prefixes older than at
least 24 hours qualify. Empty prefixes are supported; full-length corrupt,
oversized, replaced, locked, linked, reparse and invalid/foreign proof objects
remain untouched.

Current or needed hashes remain protected. A duplicate prefix can retire while
its hash is referenced only when a complete independent replacement matches
root/hash/length/native ID, is durably cataloged, and remains held by a read
guard through FULL completion. The replacement ID is journaled and revalidated
after restart. Unknown or changed replacements do not authorize deletion.

Ordinary capture recovery defers proofs with pending retirements. Retirement
removes guarded data, proves its absence, removes only the matching proof,
proves absence again and FULL-completes. Every native boundary is recoverable.
New references cancel present work unless a verified replacement still exists.
Already missing data completes with readiness withheld when no replacement
survives; needed work remains. A missing proof does not discard durable ownership
while its prefix is still needed. Changed bytes or proof IDs preserve both data
and journal authority with visible retry diagnostics.

Old completed-block deletion journals recover before new capture work. When
captures defer, present block deletions cancel without deleting bytes; already
missing blocks reconcile. This avoids capacity deadlock and stale readiness.
Pending rows and each pass share a 1,000-operation bound across both journal
kinds. References refresh after capture changes, and unresolved captures still
prevent new completed-block sweeps. Logs use stable capture GUIDs, counts,
byte counts, exception types, HResult and native/SQLite codes, without names,
paths, hashes, content or SQL exception text.
