The offline-owner forwarding regression initially failed on its RW variant:
both visible payloads were correct and case/needed work drained, but received
TOPIC and TOPIC/NESTED publication queues remained forever. Child conflict
resolution had superseded the signed roots' original child envelopes. The RO
variant passed without acquiring signing authority.

Schema 33 persists exact original graph/capture/epoch-bound verified prefixes.
RW publication may rebase after every original direct child has an exact or
causally dominating signed local version. Missing, concurrent, unsigned,
unverified and equal-vector/different-content children remain pending. Signed
tombstones and dominating kind changes represent obsolete children/descendants.
Signed and stored deletion/kind/content fields must agree. No missing member
implies deletion. Complete original graph proof, native guards, current epoch
and existing FULL commit guards precede replacement; queue/draft/proof release
is atomic. RO continues to use exact received versions without signing.

Capture and proof share the bounded publication budget. A one-unit pass can
resume a completed capture and advance one proof member. Prefixes survive
restart, mutations reset their epoch authority, and exceptional work consumes
its offered budget. Diagnostics identify pending/completed rebase by queue
session and counts, with no child paths, hashes or envelope bodies.
